NovaCraftZ — NEWSPAPER FRONT PAGES
==================================
Every picture in this folder (.png / .jpg / .webp) is one newspaper front page.
Hold a Folded Newspaper and right-click: you read one of them, picked at random (never the same one twice in a row).
Put 10-20 in here for local headlines, world news, ads, lost-and-found… whatever brings the world to life.
A tall page reads best (about 600 x 840 or bigger). news_01-03.png are examples — replace them or keep them.

THEN: double-click "Rebuild index.bat" (it lists them in index.json and packs them into index.html + the Windows exe).
